Types of Networks

Networks can be classified by the area they cover.

🏠

LAN

Local Area Network

A LAN connects computers and devices in a small area such as a home, school, or office.

Small Area
🏙️

MAN

Metropolitan Area Network

A MAN connects multiple LANs across a city or large campus.

City Area
🌍

WAN

Wide Area Network

A WAN connects networks over very large areas, including different cities and countries.

Worldwide
